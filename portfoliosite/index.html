My journey

My Early Life and Education Up to Now

My School Life

I was born on April 1, 2004, in a small town in North Waziristan in a family with limited resources. I started my education at Government Primary School Mama Ziarat when I was five years old. For three years, I received top-position awards. After completing grade four, I left my home for further education and joined Government High School Data Khel, where I completed grade five.

I later returned to my village and joined Islamia Public School Mama Ziarat, now known as Sun Rise Public School Mama Ziarat, where I completed grade seven. Financial difficulties caused me to pause my education for one year. In 2018, I received a fully funded scholarship at Army Public School Thal. I studied there from grade eight to matriculation. In 2021, I completed matriculation with 1006 out of 1100 marks from the Federal Board and was the institution's second-position holder.

My College Life

In 2021, I joined Army Public School and College Kohat Cantt for college. I worked hard in my first-year examinations and achieved grade A. I studied second year in Kohat and completed my FSc Pre-Engineering from D&K Ashraf's College Mirali with grade A in 2023.

My University Life

In 2023, I started teaching at Sun Rise Public School because I could not afford university expenses. I then received another fully funded scholarship at Akhuwat College Kasur in the BS Information Technology department. The diverse university environment has helped me become more confident in sharing ideas with people from different cultures and languages. I am now in my sixth semester and, Insha-Allah, will complete my degree in 2027.

My Soft Skills

I value honesty, discipline, respect, and responsibility in my personal, educational, and social life. I have strong communication skills, especially in English, and I take deadlines seriously. I enjoy solving technical and social problems by trying different approaches until I find a practical solution. I also adapt quickly to new environments and technologies, and I enjoy working with team members while respecting their opinions.

My Timetable and Health Activities

I wake up early for prayer and exercise for about half an hour. My classes start at 8:30 AM and continue until 5 PM. In the evening, I spend time with friends, have dinner, and study in the IT lab. After Isha prayer, I return to the lab for further study until midnight. This routine helps me balance my health, education, and personal time.
